About
New Generation Baptist Church

Welcome to New Generation Baptist Church

New Generation Baptist Church is a Christ-centered community dedicated to sharing the love of God and nurturing spiritual growth in every believer. Since our beginning, we have sought to be a light in our community—encouraging faith, hope, and unity through worship, discipleship, and outreach.

We believe church is more than a building; it’s a family. Together, we learn, serve, and grow in grace, equipping one another to live out the gospel in everyday life.

OUR MISSION

To glorify God by reaching out to our community with the gospel, nurturing believers through sound teaching, and equipping them to serve with love and integrity.

OUR VISION

To be a Christ-centered family that inspires hope, deepens faith, and transforms lives — locally and globally — through the power of God’s Word.

CORE VALUES

FAITH

We stand firm in our trust in God, believing His word as the foundation for every step we take.

PRAYER

We depend on consistent, heartfelt prayer as the key to divine direction, power, and transformation.

LOVE

We show Christ’s love by serving others selflessly and creating a community of genuine care and compassion.

COMMUNITY

We are a family of believers, united in purpose and committed to helping one another grow in faith and life.
